# Bornless Ritual — Architecture Contract

This document is the binding contract for every module. Implementation agents build
against it; reviewers check against it. Where code and this document disagree, the
document wins unless the document is corrected in the same change.

Companion documents: `CORE_API.md` (RitualCore public Swift API, Linux-tested),
`RENDER_CONTRACT.md` (frame graph, resources, bindings, shader ownership),
`BornlessRitual/Shaders/ShaderTypes.h` (struct layouts shared by Swift and MSL).

## 1. Repository layout

```
BornlessRitual.xcodeproj/          generated by Tools/gen_xcodeproj.rb (committed)
BornlessRitual/                    iOS app target (Swift + Metal); NOT buildable on Linux
  App/                             SwiftUI app, views, debug panel, timeline, settings model
  Render/                          Renderer, passes, scene, resources (Metal only)
  Shaders/                         *.metal, *.h (ShaderTypes.h is the Swift<->MSL contract)
  Interaction/                     gestures, haptics, narration
  Capture/                         capture harness (launch args -> PNG/clip/JSON)
  Resources/                       Assets.xcassets, RitualText.json
  Info.plist, BornlessRitual-Bridging-Header.h
Packages/RitualCore/               SwiftPM package, pure Swift + Foundation, builds and tests on Linux
docs/                              this contract, sources, critic protocol, rounds log
Tools/                             gen_xcodeproj.rb, metal-lint/, truth/, capture/
```

Rules:
- `Packages/RitualCore` imports only `Foundation`. No `simd`, no `Metal`, no `UIKit`
  (`simd` is unavailable on Linux). It defines its own `RVec2`/`RVec3` (Double).
- The app converts `RVec3` -> `SIMD3<Float>` at the boundary (`Render/CoreBridge.swift`).
- No Unity/Unreal/SceneKit/RealityKit anywhere. ModelIO/ImageIO/CoreHaptics/AVFoundation
  are allowed for non-render-path work. MetalFX and MetalPerformanceShaders are allowed.
- All randomness in simulation and rendering derives from `seed` (see §6).

## 2. Units, axes, scene layout

- Right-handed, metres, **+Y up**. **East = +X, North = −Z, South = +Z, West = −X**.
- Chamber: interior 6 m (X) × 4 m (Y) × 6 m (Z), centred at origin, floor at y = 0.
  Walls rough stone; floor polished dark flagstone (glossy, roughness ≈ 0.18) with a
  chalk double circle (radii 1.45 m and 1.60 m) and the daemon's name in chalk at the
  four quarter marks.
- Quarter candles on the circle axis at radius 1.80 m: East (1.8, 0, 0), South (0, 0, 1.8),
  West (−1.8, 0, 0), North (0, 0, −1.8). Each: wax cylinder r = 0.035 m, h = 0.22 m on an
  iron stand (h = 0.90 m); flame origin at y = 1.13 m.
- Altar: stone block 1.0 × 0.85 × 0.5 m at (0, 0, −2.4); two altar candles (r 0.025,
  h 0.18) at (±0.35, 0.85, −2.4); incense censer at (0, 0.85, −2.3) emitting smoke.
- Sorcerer: stands at (0, 0, 0.75) facing −Z (toward altar). Height 1.80 m. Hooded robe;
  hands and face use the SSS skin material; face is inside the hood's shadow.
- Sigil: floats at (0, 1.55, 0), plane horizontal (normal +Y), outer ring radius 0.75 m.
- Daemon: condenses at (0, 1.15, −0.25), final height ≈ 2.4 m, facing +Z (toward sorcerer).
- Camera orbit target default (0, 1.0, 0). Camera presets (position → target):
  `front` (0, 1.5, 4.6) → (0, 1.2, 0); `threequarter` (3.3, 1.9, 3.3) → (0, 1.2, 0);
  `profile` (4.6, 1.4, 0) → (0, 1.2, 0); `overhead` (0, 3.85, 0.05) → (0, 0, 0);
  `low` (0, 0.32, 3.6) → (0, 1.3, 0); `closeup` (0.9, 1.65, 1.35) → (0, 1.45, −0.1).
  Vertical FOV 50°, near 0.05 m, far 30 m, reversed-Z depth (1 = near).
- Quarter azimuths (camera yaw, degrees, measured from +Z toward +X, i.e. yaw of the
  camera's forward vector): East 90, South 0, West 270, North 180. "Facing a quarter"
  = |wrap(yaw − quarterYaw)| ≤ 30°. Yaw is the camera's forward direction, so orbiting
  the camera to the west side of the room and looking east faces East.

## 3. Ritual stages (order is fixed and asserted in tests)

| # | Stage id | Mini-game | Completion effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `oath` | hold to chant ≥ 3.0 s (progress decays 0.5/s when released) | floor ring kindles (ember glow along chalk circle, intensity = progress) |
| 2 | `air` (East) | face East ±30°, trace the Air sigil path | East candle ignites, pale white-gold (K 2800 tint + white-blue core) |
| 3 | `fire` (South) | face South, trace Fire | South candle ignites, deep red-orange (1500 K) |
| 4 | `water` (West) | face West, trace Water | West candle ignites, blue (CuCl-like blue-green emission) |
| 5 | `earth` (North) | face North, trace Earth | North candle ignites, green (BaCl-like green) |
| 6 | `spirit` | rhythm taps on 6 barbarous names: Aoth Abaoth Basum Isak Sabaoth Iao | fiery sigil erupts above the circle |
| 7 | `sigilSpin` | flick to spin; spin energy charges manifestation | when charge ≥ 1 the manifestation begins |
| 8 | `manifestation` | none (watch); daemon condenses over 8 s then idles | ritual complete |

Trace paths (normalised [0,1]² polylines, defined in RitualCore `SigilTemplates`):
Air = upward triangle with a horizontal bar through it; Fire = upward triangle;
Water = downward triangle; Earth = downward triangle with a bar. Scoring: the template
is resampled to 24 checkpoints; the user's trace hits checkpoints **in order** when a
trace point falls within 0.09 (normalised) of the next unhit checkpoint; success when
≥ 18 of 24 are hit when the finger lifts. Failure resets the trace (no penalty).

Rhythm: beats at stage-relative times 1.0, 1.8, 2.6, 3.4, 4.2, 5.0 s (one name per
beat, 75 BPM). A tap within ±0.15 s = perfect, ±0.30 s = good, else miss. ≥ 5 good
of 6 → success; otherwise the six beats repeat from 6.5 s after the last beat.

Sigil spin: rings i = 0..4 with radii 0.75, 0.62, 0.50, 0.39, 0.29 m, ember filament
rune rings (the Hebrew name letters and the kamea path are inscribed on rings 0 and 2).
Dynamics in `SigilDynamics` (see CORE_API): rigid rings with I_i = m_i r_i², coupled
so adjacent rings counter-rotate (coupling torque k(ω_i + ω_{i+1})), viscous + Coulomb
friction, flick impulse applied to the ring under the finger (or ring 0 if none).
Sparks shed tangentially with speed |ω| r + jitter; spark spawn rate ∝ |ω| r.
Spin energy E = Σ ½ I ω². Manifest charge: dq/dt = E / E_ref, E_ref = 1.2 J·s;
charge clamps to [0,1]; stage 8 begins at charge ≥ 1. Friction slider scales both
friction coefficients; gravity slider scales g for embers.

Manifestation: `manifestT` rises 0 → 1 over 8.0 s (smoothstep); the daemon's SDF
condenses out of the sigil's fire and smoke (noise threshold sweeps with manifestT),
then presence loop: breathing 0.12 Hz, mane fire, slow head turn ("dominant, unhurried").

## 5. Time, ticks, timeline

- Simulation runs at a **fixed 120 Hz** (`Tick = Int`, dt = 1/120 s). Ritual time
  T (seconds) advances per display frame by `wallDt × timeScale` unless paused.
  Ticks to simulate = floor(T·120) − currentTick. Rendering uses the sim state at the
  latest integer tick (no interpolation).
- Keyframes every 240 ticks (2 s) hold the entire mutable sim state (`Keyframe`, a few
  hundred bytes). Rewind/scrub = restore nearest keyframe ≤ target and replay the input
  log to the target tick. Forward scrub beyond the max simulated tick simulates forward.
- All continuous effects (smoke density, flame shape, ember trajectories, sorcerer
  animation, daemon animation) are **pure functions of (tick, seed, sim state)**;
  embers use closed-form linear-drag ballistics from their spawn tick (§6).

## 6. Determinism

- `SeededRNG` = PCG32 (seed, stream). `Hash.u32(seed, a, b, c)` = xxhash-style 32-bit
  mix; `Hash.unit(...)` maps to [0,1). CPU sim uses only these. GPU shaders use the same
  hash (`hash_u32` in `Common.h`, bit-identical to Swift's `Hash.u32`) keyed by
  (seed, frameIndex or tick, pixel/particle id). A test in RitualCore pins hash outputs.
- Ember i spawned at tick s from ring state R(s): initial position/velocity from R(s)
  and `Hash(seed, s, i)`. Position at time t: linear drag k = 6.0 s⁻¹ (mm-scale glowing
  particle, Stokes regime approximation), g scaled by the gravity slider:
  v_t = g/k (terminal); x(t) = x0 + v_t τ + (v0 − v_t)(1 − e^{−kτ})/k, τ = t − s/120;
  plus a curl-noise displacement d(x0, τ, seed) ≤ 4 cm for turbulence. Lifetime 2.5 s,
  temperature cools 1900 K → 800 K (blackbody colour), brightness ∝ T⁴ normalised.
- Render randomness: `frameIndex` is a `UInt32`. Live: increments per rendered frame.
  After any seek/pause-scrub and in capture mode: the renderer resets temporal history
  and renders K = 16 warm-up frames with frameIndex = tick·16 + k; the 16th is displayed
  or captured. Identical (seed, tick, camera, settings, renderPath) ⇒ bit-identical image
  on the same device.
- Floating-point: no `fast-math` on the CPU sim (Swift default); GPU uses Metal fast math
  (identical across runs on the same GPU).

## 7. Capture harness (both critic and developer use it)

Launch arguments (UserDefaults style, `-key value`):
`-stage 1..8`, `-camera front|threequarter|profile|overhead|low|closeup`,
`-renderPath rt|fallback|auto`, `-seed <uint64>`, `-capture stills|clip|perf|none`,
`-clipSeconds 5`, `-perfSeconds 300`, `-runName <string>`, `-renderScale 0.67`,
`-autopilot 1|0`, `-warmup 16`, `-narration 0|1`.
Behaviour: autopilot (RitualCore `Autopilot`) plays the ritual deterministically to the
stage's showcase tick (`Autopilot.showcaseTick(stage)`), the renderer performs the
warm-up, then:
- `stills`: writes `<run>/still_s<stage>_<camera>_<path>.png` at native drawable resolution.
- `clip`: writes `<run>/clip_s<stage>_<camera>_<path>/frame_%05d.png` for clipSeconds×60
  frames while the sim runs live from the showcase tick (still deterministic).
- `perf`: runs the full ritual on autopilot for perfSeconds, logs every frame.
Always writes `<run>/frametime.json` (schema: `FrameLog` in CORE_API) and
`<run>/manifest.json` (config, device, chart report, name, sigil cells).
Files live in the app's Documents container: `Documents/Captures/<runName>/`.
Host side: `Tools/capture/critic_capture.sh <device-id>` runs the 24 stills × 2 paths and
the two clips × 2 paths, pulls with `xcrun devicectl device copy from`, and
`Tools/capture/assemble.sh` makes MP4s with ffmpeg. Metal RT does not run in the
Simulator; the scripts refuse simulator destinations.

## 8. Debug panel (SwiftUI overlay, toggled by a gear button)

Shows: fps (1 s average), frame time ms (CPU + GPU), 1% low, `ProcessInfo.thermalState`,
active render path and why (device capability), render scale, MetalFX on/off, stage and
progress, chart report (Appendix A format, monospaced), daemon name (Hebrew + Latin) and
sigil preview (2D `Path`), sliders: flame intensity (0–3, default 1), smoke density
(0–3, 1), ember count (0–3, 1), sigil friction (0–3, 1), gravity (0–3, 1), candle warmth
(0–1, 0.5 → 1500–2400 K), ambient light (0–1, 0.15), exposure (−4–+4 EV, 0), time scale
(0–4, 1); toggles: narration, haptics, autopilot, pause; timeline scrubber; stage jump;
render path picker (auto/rt/fallback; switching rebuilds pipelines, no relaunch).

## 9. Performance budget (iPhone 15 Pro, 60 fps sustained 5 min)

Internal render scale default 0.67 (≥ 0.6 permitted), MetalFX temporal to native.
Target GPU time ≤ 13.5 ms: G-buffer 1.5, direct lighting + shadows (1 shadow ray/px,
light chosen by importance) 2.0, floor reflections (1 ray/px on glossy pixels) 1.5,
denoise (temporal + 3 à-trous) 2.0, froxels 160×90×64 1.8, SSS 0.6, flames/sigil/embers
1.0, daemon SDF march (bounded) 1.5, composite 0.3, MetalFX 1.5, bloom/shimmer/tonemap
0.8. The debug panel exposes render scale and froxel depth to trade quality for thermal
headroom. CPU: sim ≤ 0.5 ms/frame; acceleration-structure refit only for the sorcerer.
